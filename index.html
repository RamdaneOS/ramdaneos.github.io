04

Data & Methods

Risk measurement work sits at the centre of what I do: building indices, scoring methodologies, and geospatial and text-based analytics that turn heterogeneous country data into something an investment committee can act on.

Contextual Risk Index (IFC)

Rebuilt the methodology, indicator set and scoring for IFC's global screening tool, spanning 200+ countries and integrating climate, governance, fragility and socio-political indicators into investment risk scores used by investment teams. Includes the annual update cycle.

Research methods

  • Panel econometrics in R
  • Deep-learning land-cover classification (Sentinel-2, Google Earth Engine)
  • NLP pipelines for indicator derivation — word embeddings, GloVe, t-SNE
  • Geospatial exposure mapping and heat-maps
  • Systematic review, survey design, qualitative coding (MaxQDA)
  • Proxy-based quantitative frameworks for non-observable social dimensions in data-scarce contexts

Tools

R (advanced) · Python · SQL · ArcGIS · Google Earth Engine · Power BI
